Privacy Policy

Zikra: İbadet Takibi — effective as of 2026-08-18

Türkçe

This privacy policy applies to the Zikra: İbadet Takibi mobile application ("Application"), developed by Nornir Devs ("we", "us", "the Service Provider"), established in Türkiye. We are the data controller for the processing described here. The Application is offered free of charge and is provided "AS IS".

1. Information We Collect

a) Account information. To use cloud backup, multi-device sync, and prayer-tracking history, you may create an account using your email address (passwordless magic-link or email/password) or by signing in with your Google account. Creating an account is optional — most core features (prayer times, Qibla, Qur'an reading tracker, dua library) work without one. If you create an account, you may optionally add your first name, last name, birth date, gender, and city from the Profile screen; none of these fields are required.

b) Location data. The Application requests precise device location (GPS) to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla (Kaaba) direction for where you are, and to suggest your country and prayer-time calculation method during onboarding. Location is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers. Where prayer times are retrieved from an official regional service, the request is routed through our server and the response is cached by city — that cache is not linked to your account or to any user identifier.

c) Worship records (special category data). If you sign in and give the explicit consent described in section 2, the following content is stored on our servers (Supabase) solely to back it up and sync it across your devices: daily prayer completion records, missed-prayer (qada) counters, dhikr counters, and Qur'an reading (hatim) progress. Because these records show how you practise your religion, we treat them as special category personal data (sensitive data revealing religious belief). This data is private to your account and protected by row-level access rules. If you do not give that consent, none of it leaves your device.

d) Automatically collected information. Certain technical information is collected automatically: device type, OS version, and crash/diagnostic reports (via Firebase Crashlytics); app usage and performance data (via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring); and advertising identifier / ad interaction data (via Google AdMob). Ads shown in the Application may be personalized: Google may use your advertising identifier and ad interaction data to select ads it considers more relevant to you. Section 4 explains how you can review, change, or withdraw this.

2. Legal Basis and Your Explicit Consent

We process your personal data on the following bases:

  • Your explicit consent — for the worship records in section 1(c). Turkish law (KVKK art. 6) treats data revealing religious belief as special category data requiring explicit consent, and the GDPR (art. 9) sets the same requirement. We therefore ask for this consent separately, in its own screen, before any worship record is sent to our servers. It is never bundled into acceptance of terms, and nothing is pre-ticked.
  • Performance of the service you request — for account information (section 1(a)), so that you can sign in and use cloud backup.
  • Our legitimate interest in a working app — for crash and performance diagnostics (section 1(d)), used to find and fix faults.
  • Your consent — for personalized advertising, collected through Google's certified consent form where required (see section 4).

Refusing is a real option. If you decline the worship-records consent, the Application keeps working exactly as before; your records simply stay on your device and are never uploaded. You will not be able to restore them on a new phone or after reinstalling.

Withdrawing. You can withdraw this consent at any time from Profile → Account inside the Application. When you withdraw, syncing stops immediately and the worship records already stored on our servers are deleted. The copies on your own device are not touched. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.

3. Does the Application Use Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

The Application does not currently use AI technologies to process your personal data. If an AI-powered feature is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand to describe it.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy. They process data on our behalf as processors, except where they act as independent controllers for their own advertising purposes.

  • Supabase (backend, authentication, database — this is where account information and, with your consent, worship records are stored)
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring)
  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob
  • Expo

Firebase Authentication is not used; sign-in is handled by Supabase.

International transfers. Your account information and — with your explicit consent — your worship records are stored through Supabase on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (AWS eu-central-1). Firebase/Google and Expo services may also process data on their infrastructure outside your country. If you are located in Türkiye, this constitutes a transfer of personal data abroad under Article 9 of the KVKK. As of the date of this policy, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board has not issued an adequacy decision for any country; transfers take place under our service providers’ data processing commitments and technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit and at rest. If you do not want your records stored this way, you may withhold the worship-records consent or withdraw it at any time, in which case your records stay on your device only.

Advertising and your consent. The Application is free of charge and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ad content is limited to the “General audiences” rating, and ads may be personalized as described in section 1(d). Where required by local law, we ask for your consent through Google’s certified consent form before any ad is loaded, and you can review, change, or withdraw that choice at any time from Profile → Ad Privacy inside the Application. Wherever you are located, you can also opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier from your device’s system settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Declining personalization does not remove ads; it only makes them non-personalized.

Worship records are never shared with advertising services, and we do not use them to target ads.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information we collect: as required by law (e.g. to comply with a subpoena); when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud; or with service providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations and do not use it for their own independent purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you, and obtain a copy of it;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete — profile fields can be edited directly in the Application;
  • Delete your account and all associated data (see below);
  • Withdraw consent — for worship records (Profile → Account) or for personalized ads (Profile → Ad Privacy), at any time and without giving a reason;
  • Object to or restrict processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. In Türkiye this is the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at zikrauygulama@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

Account deletion. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In-app: Profile → Delete My Account
  • Web: https://ozgurrtuncc.github.io/zikra-legal/delete-account/

Deletion is permanent and immediate: your profile, prayer records, qada counters, dhikr counters, and Qur'an reading progress are all removed from our servers. Uninstalling the Application alone does not delete your account or cloud data — please use one of the options above.

6. Data Retention

  • Account information and worship records: kept for as long as your account exists. Worship records are deleted from our servers as soon as you withdraw the consent in section 2, even if you keep your account.
  • Analytics data (Firebase): event-level data is retained for 2 months; user-level data for 14 months.
  • Crash and performance reports: retained by Google per Firebase's standard retention settings.
  • Prayer-time cache: short-lived and not linked to any user.

You can request deletion of your account data at any time as described in section 5, or contact us at zikrauygulama@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information should contact us at zikrauygulama@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards — including row-level database access controls and encrypted connections — to protect the information we process. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application. You may also limit ad personalization and manage location permission from your device's system settings at any time, and review or withdraw your advertising consent from Profile → Ad Privacy inside the Application.
