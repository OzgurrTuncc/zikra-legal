Privacy Policy

Zikra: İbadet Takibi — effective as of 2026-07-26

This privacy policy applies to the Zikra: İbadet Takibi mobile application ("Application"), developed by Nornir Devs ("we", "us", "the Service Provider"). The Application is offered free of charge and is provided "AS IS".

1. Information We Collect

a) Account information. To use cloud backup, multi-device sync, and prayer-tracking history, you may create an account using your email address (passwordless magic-link or email/password) or by signing in with your Google account. Creating an account is optional — most core features (prayer times, Qibla, Qur'an reading tracker, dua library) work without one. If you create an account, you may optionally add your first name, last name, birth date, gender, and city from the Profile screen; none of these fields are required.

b) Location data. The Application requests precise device location (GPS) to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla (Kaaba) direction for where you are, and to suggest your country and prayer-time calculation method during onboarding. Location is processed on your device and is not stored on our servers.

c) Content you create. If you sign in, the following content is stored on our servers (Supabase) solely to back it up and sync it across your devices: daily prayer completion records, missed-prayer (qada) counters, and Qur'an reading (hatim) progress. This data is private to your account and protected by row-level access rules.

d) Automatically collected information. Certain technical information is collected automatically: device type, OS version, and crash/diagnostic reports (via Firebase Crashlytics); app usage and performance data (via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring); and advertising identifier / ad interaction data (via Google AdMob). Ads shown in the Application may be personalized: Google may use your advertising identifier and ad interaction data to select ads it considers more relevant to you. Section 3 explains how you can review, change, or withdraw this.

2. Does the Application Use Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

The Application does not currently use AI technologies to process your personal data. If an AI-powered feature is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand to describe it.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase (backend, authentication, database)
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring)
  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In
  • Google AdMob
  • Expo

Advertising and your consent. The Application is free of charge and shows ads through Google AdMob. Ad content is limited to the “General audiences” rating, and ads may be personalized as described in section 1(d). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google’s certified consent form before any ad is loaded, and you can review, change, or withdraw that choice at any time from Profile → Ad Privacy inside the Application. Wherever you are located, you can also opt out of ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier from your device’s system settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Declining personalization does not remove ads; it only makes them non-personalized.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information we collect: as required by law (e.g. to comply with a subpoena); when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud; or with service providers who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations and do not use it for their own independent purposes.

4. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, in either of these ways:

  • In-app: Profile → Delete My Account
  • Web: https://ozgurrtuncc.github.io/zikra-legal/delete-account/

Deletion is permanent and immediate: your profile, prayer records, qada counters, and Qur'an reading progress are all removed from our servers. Uninstalling the Application alone does not delete your account or cloud data — please use one of the options above.

5. Data Retention

Content you provide is retained for as long as your account exists. Automatically collected diagnostic/analytics data is retained by our providers (Firebase) per their standard retention settings. You can request deletion of your account data at any time as described above, or contact us at zikrauygulama@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided us with personal information should contact us at zikrauygulama@gmail.com.

7. Security

We use reasonable physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards — including row-level database access controls and encrypted connections — to protect the information we process. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Opt-Out

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the Application. You may also limit ad personalization and manage location permission from your device's system settings at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you can additionally review or withdraw your advertising consent from Profile → Ad Privacy inside the Application.
